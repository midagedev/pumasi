          How to read the map
          • Hall = a host. Card bars: what runners hold, solid and striped together; the blue stripes are the part ready runners give way to a waiting job; others (grey); free. White line = used now. CPU×1.5 = cores shared.
          • Machine = a runner doing one job; its floor is what it holds, its tank what it uses now.
          • Blue, READY = a runner up with no job yet. It takes its class's next job at once; striped floor = room it gives up for another class's waiting job. AHEAD = one started for a job that needs another: up as that one ends, before the job comes.
          • Crate = a waiting job. Redder = longer; pink = expedited.
          • Belt = a class (one runs-on label); its jobs queue there.
          • Pallets = finished: green ok, red failed.
